The details

Before you book.

What’s included, what’s separate, and what happens after launch.

Is ad spend included?

No. The package pays for the setup and the included management. Your advertising budget is separate and paid by your business.

What counts as handoff for Full System?

We walk you through the completed site and verify that a test inquiry goes through the agreed follow-up and booking flow. The balance isn’t tied to the end of the 60-day management period.

What happens if I skip the $500/month plan?

Ignite stops managing your ads once the included period ends, or when you cancel ongoing management. We’ll confirm who will be responsible for the campaigns next.

Does quarterly content support include another shoot?

No. The setup includes one shoot day and the initial batch of edits. Any return visit and travel are quoted separately; quarterly editing and content planning do not include ad management.
